Big12ology

The study of the Big 12. Unofficial fan-built tools and data projects about the sixteen-team league — its standings math, its stadiums, and its chaos.

Tiebreaker Tracker live

The official Big 12 tiebreaking procedures applied to live results after every game — projected championship matchup, step-by-step tie narratives, and a what-if simulator with five rating models.

Attendance Tracker live

Weekly home-game attendance for all sixteen teams — capacity-sourced stadium data, fill rates, and hand-verified box scores where the feeds fall short.

Preview Machine annual

A season preview built by pipeline, not vibes: roster continuity, QB modeling, schedule chains, and the Chain rating that powers the tiebreaker simulator's house model.
