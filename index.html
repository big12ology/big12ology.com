The study of the Big 12. Unofficial fan-built tools and data projects about the sixteen-team league — its standings math, its stadiums, and its chaos.

Tiebreaker Tracker live

The official Big 12 tiebreaking procedures applied to live results after every game — projected championship matchup, step-by-step tie narratives, clinch and elimination proofs, and a what-if simulator with four public rating models. The 2024 and 2025 seasons are kept alongside the live one: replay a year week by week, or rewrite a result and watch the rules answer.

Attendance Tracker live

Weekly home-game attendance for all sixteen teams, back to 2012 — capacity-sourced stadium data, fill rates, sellout streaks, kickoff-window and weather effects, and hand-verified box scores where the feeds fall short.

Tie Archaeology evergreen

Every final-standings tie in the modern Big 12, 2011–2025, broken step by step by the tiebreaker engine — plus the all-time head-to-head grid for the current sixteen.
