Pools new

2 season-long games · 1 account

Two season-long games, one account. The pick'em takes every Big 12 game against the spread on one line for the whole room, frozen when the week is published and never moved again. Survivor takes one team a week and never the same team twice. Both lock at the first kickoff, and what you picked becomes public then and not before. The boards rank the season with pushes counted as pushes, next to the chalk — what simply taking every favorite would have scored, which is a harder benchmark than it sounds.
