Step six, and why it matters that it has never fired

The published procedure has seven steps. The first five are arithmetic you can check: head-to-head, common opponents, the standings walk, strength of schedule, total wins. Anyone with the results can verify them, which is what this site does.

Step six is not. It is a SportSource Analytics team rating — a commercial product. The conference buys it. It is not published, it cannot be recomputed from public results, and there is no version of it a reader can audit. If a Big 12 championship-game berth ever turns on step six, the answer to "why this team?" is a number nobody outside the transaction can see.

Step seven is a coin toss, which is at least honest about being arbitrary.

In 40 tie groups across fifteen seasons, step six has never been reached. The deepest any tie has gone is (e) total wins , which has settled 2 of them — one rung above the part you cannot inspect. That is the whole margin: the procedure has stayed auditable so far by luck of the results, not by design.
